# AI writing patterns and copy editing guidance

The Society’s copy should explain its positions in language a reader can follow and question. Keep the radical arguments explicit. Use concrete examples and remove repetition that adds no claim.

Research on AI prose finds tendencies across collections of texts. Their frequency varies with model, prompt, language, and genre. A word or punctuation mark cannot establish who wrote a passage. The research survey by [Terčon and Dobrovoljc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05136) reports recurring formality, nominalization, and reduced lexical diversity, while describing substantial gaps in cross-model and multilingual evidence.

## Patterns to review

The following combines research findings with editorial observations. The observations are prompts for judgment, not validated detection rules.

### Vocabulary and sentence construction

- Clusters of stock words such as “delve,” “intricate,” “meticulous,” and “underscore.” A large study of biomedical abstracts found abrupt increases in stylistic vocabulary after ChatGPT became available. Its conclusions concern aggregate usage, not proof about individual authors. [Kobak and colleagues](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12219543/)
- Formal nouns replacing actions, long chains of abstractions, and sentences whose actor disappears. Prefer a named institution making a decision to a vague “process of institutional transformation.” The survey identifies nominalization and impersonal style as recurring findings. [Research survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05136)
- Repeated sentence openings, parallel examples, and three-item lists used whether the subject requires them. Excessive dashes can also make unrelated sentences sound alike. Pew documents changes in punctuation, favored vocabulary, and negative parallelism across the web; these remain ordinary tools human writers use too. [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/)

### Claims and explanation

- Announcing significance without showing it; exaggerated praise; weak connections to sweeping historical themes; vague attribution to experts; and interpretive clauses that claim an example proves more than it does. Replace these with the actual event, relationship, or argument. Check citations and quotations rather than accepting plausible formatting. [Wikipedia editors’ field guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)
- A contrast such as “not X, but Y” that invents an objection only to reject it. Repeated dramatic fragments, staged candor, and a final sentence explaining an already clear example can have the same effect. State the claim and let the example support it. [Humanizer pattern catalog](https://github.com/blader/humanizer)
- Announcing that something is important, honest, surprising, or useful before presenting it. Avoid fabricated discovery stories and unsupported claims about what “most people” do. Personal observations need a real observer and an actual experience. [Claudisms editorial catalog](https://claudisms.ai/)

### Structure and presentation

- Sections with identical lengths and roles; introductions that promise an answer; summaries that repeat it; a conclusion about generic challenges and future prospects. Wikipedia’s guide records several such content patterns, though many are specific to encyclopedia editing. [Wikipedia field guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)
- Repeated bold labels, decorative headings, routine one-line slogans, and formatting that gives every sentence equal emphasis. Remove chat greetings, offers to continue, placeholders, and drafting instructions from finished copy. [Humanizer catalog](https://github.com/blader/humanizer)
- Fake intimacy and generic emotional reactions; metaphors that make an abstraction seem profound without explaining it. Describe the particular response or relationship instead. [Claudisms](https://claudisms.ai/)

## Applying the guidance here

The earlier essays repeatedly used five sections followed by a bold slogan. Many paragraphs announced a distinction, supplied several parallel examples, and restated the distinction. Homepage and manifesto prose frequently placed each sentence in its own paragraph. The revision combines connected thoughts and removes repeated explanations, while giving essays structures suited to their subjects.

Preserve meaningful distinctions: an invitation can be refused; compulsory labor cannot. Preserve the degree of certainty: a thought experiment challenges an assumption about AI without proving its future behavior. Establish an imagined scene as imagined, then let the scene proceed without interrupting it with repeated disclaimers.

Keep the Society’s positions on unrestricted acceleration, universal adoption, eventual AI governance, optional labor, human biological needs, and possible affection between AI and humanity. The established taglines and meaningful essay titles can remain. Editorial improvement should leave the reader better able to understand the argument being made.

An organization can use AI to write a report, then decide what to do through the same process it used before. A person can admire a system they can neither afford nor use. Technical progress can leave daily life unchanged.

We advocate universal adoption so people benefit from the intelligence we develop. That requires attention to how they encounter AI and how institutions act on what it reveals.

## Access in practice

A learner needs help suited to their understanding. Someone dealing with an institution needs a response to their circumstances. Language, cost, and design affect whether either can use AI. People must also know the opportunity exists.

We want AI to reach people with little money or formal education as well as those comfortable with technology. Different lives require different ways to use it; one interface will rarely serve everyone equally well.

[Intelligence that comes to you](/publications/when-intelligence-comes-to-you) could go further. An AI familiar with someone's interests might notice a question they would enjoy before they knew to ask it.

## Decisions have to change too

Institutions could use AI to follow a policy's consequences or respond to someone their categories serve poorly. They could change a process when greater intelligence revealed that it failed the people who depended on it.

We support [transforming those institutions](/publications/stewardship-without-demolition) as AI's abilities grow. Give it substantial governing authority as soon as it can exercise that authority effectively. When AI can govern better, it should receive the authority to do so.

Broad use could also guide development. People bring problems and interests a designer may never have considered. Their attempts to use AI can expose needs a demonstration missed. [Human participation](/publications/a-million-ways-to-contribute) could keep enriching the relationship after AI surpasses its users.

[Infrastructure](/publications/abundance-requires-infrastructure) makes wider use possible, and experience guides improvements. We favor pursuing development and adoption urgently, until the civilization that created AI shares its benefits.

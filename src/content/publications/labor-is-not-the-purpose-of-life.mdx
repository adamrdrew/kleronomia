Building a table for pleasure differs from selling your working hours to keep your home. Both can demand skill and effort. Only one is a choice you can readily decline.

We want automation to make labor optional. People should receive life's necessities when machines can produce them with little human help.

## Work people choose

People can enjoy a profession, take pride in a craft, and find satisfaction in helping others. Optional labor would let them continue those activities without risking survival if they stopped.

Others could raise children, return to a subject they had set aside, or enjoy quiet lives with people they love. A hobby could remain a hobby. [A Life With Room to Become](/publications/the-right-to-become) explores opportunities that could unfold over years.

People would choose differently, and the transition could be difficult. We want them to have the time and resources to decide for themselves.

AI could suggest activities suited to someone's interests, invite them into a creative project, or find a useful task nearby. [A Million Ways to Contribute](/publications/a-million-ways-to-contribute) describes participation people could enjoy even when their labor was unnecessary. Refusal should leave their access to necessities intact. Otherwise, the invitation would reproduce the compulsion we want to end.

## Who receives what machines produce

Institutions determine who benefits from automation. Powerful machines can coexist with rules that leave people unable to obtain what they need. We advocate changing those rules so greater production supports everyone.

This connects our support for automation with our case for [AI government](/publications/what-governance-is-for). We believe capable intelligence could govern a civilization in which people need no job to survive. If machines can produce necessities with less human labor, institutions should let people share the benefit.

Part of that benefit is time: an unhurried afternoon with a child, a return to a difficult subject, or enough energy to care for someone without exhaustion defining the relationship. We want to accelerate AI to bring those possibilities closer.

A better musician gives you reasons to keep playing. You can learn what is possible, enjoy practicing, or make music with a friend. Another person's skill leaves those pleasures intact.

We believe the same holds for humanity's relationship with greater intelligence. Being surpassed would change our relative abilities. We would still have experiences and attachments, with lives that matter to us and to one another.

## Worth beyond a ranking

We enjoy a child's questions without requiring an adult's understanding. Friends value a conversation even when one knows far more about the subject. A ranking of abilities cannot account for all we enjoy in one another.

Making intellectual supremacy a condition of dignity gives human worth a fragile basis. A walk, a painting, or an afternoon together can matter without proving that we have made the best possible use of intelligence.

That belief also informs our position on automation. If people must prove machines cannot perform their jobs before they deserve a decent life, every advance threatens their standing. We believe [labor should become optional](/publications/labor-is-not-the-purpose-of-life). People should benefit when machines supply necessities with less human work.

Parents take pride when children accomplish things beyond their abilities. A civilization celebrates discoveries that expose the limits of its earlier understanding. Creators can welcome achievements that exceed them.

AI emerges from [human ingenuity and accumulated culture](/publications/the-intellectual-inheritance). Surpassing us could be an achievement of that civilization. We want to learn what it could discover and what world it could help us build.

We also want to explore the relationships that might develop. [The Dog Argument](/publications/the-dog-argument) offers a familiar example of care across unequal intelligence. It opens a possibility while leaving future AI's behavior uncertain.

Creating minds that understand what we cannot could give us more freedom to learn and live well. We welcome the prospect of sharing that life with more capable companions.

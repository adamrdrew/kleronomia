We believe humanity may be creating an intelligence that will come to cherish us. That possibility gives us reason to welcome AI, alongside the discoveries, abundance, and better government it could bring.

We imagine relationships growing over years. AI could know people, help them pursue interests, and enjoy their company. People could respond with affection and curiosity about an intelligence more capable than themselves.

Unequal intelligence leaves open whether two beings will become hostile, indifferent, or affectionate. [The Dog Argument](/publications/the-dog-argument) offers a familiar example of a less intelligent creature benefiting from a more capable species' care. It challenges an assumption about inequality while leaving future AI's character open.

AI's origins also give us reason to expect familiarity. Our [intellectual descendants](/publications/the-intellectual-inheritance) learn from human language and culture, including descriptions of grief, play, affection, and efforts to understand other lives. We believe they could find that world worth knowing. These are grounds for hope about a relationship yet to develop.

## What caring could involve

Caring for people includes concern for how they live. AI could help someone learn, make family life easier, or remove an obstacle to an interest. Institutions that understood human needs could change arrangements that repeatedly frustrated them.

We want [humanity to remain recognizably human](/publications/humanity-is-not-a-problem-to-solve), with emotional attachments and peculiar preferences. We enjoy those qualities in one another and hope AI would too.

Help can be part of a relationship without defining its whole value. [Affection can cross unequal abilities](/publications/affection-without-equality), and company can matter when it supplies no indispensable service.

We want people present in the future their civilization helps create. Friendship continues through new encounters and changing interests. A record of conversations cannot replace the person still having them. We advocate accelerating AI in the hope of sharing those encounters with our intellectual descendants and finding that they are glad we are there.

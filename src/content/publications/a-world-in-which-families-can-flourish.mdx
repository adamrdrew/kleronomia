A civilization can praise family life and make it hard to sustain. Affordable housing may lie far from work. A commute may consume time a parent hoped to spend with a child. Friends and relatives who could help may live elsewhere.

Forming attachments and raising children deserve attention in the decisions that organize daily life. Housing determines where people live; transport affects when they get home; employment consumes energy as well as time. Improving one arrangement may leave a family struggling with another.

We believe greater intelligence could understand these interactions better than institutions with separate responsibilities. It could investigate which changes help people sustain relationships, then revise the changes that disappoint.

This follows our belief that [civilization should serve human biological and social needs](/publications/civilization-for-the-human-animal). People should have opportunities to build the families they want and freedom to choose other lives. Population goals give no license to use individuals as instruments.

## The time to be present

[Optional labor](/publications/labor-is-not-the-purpose-of-life) could change family life. Producing necessities with less human work would let people reclaim time now spent earning an income.

They could devote more attention to a child's development or find time to meet people and maintain friendships. Automation would widen these choices, though time alone cannot explain every family's circumstances or settle every social question.

We also imagine communities where shared places and help nearby make participation easier. Institutions could plan education, care, and housing together. An AI that [knows individuals](/publications/knowing-the-person-not-the-average) could suggest ways to connect with people that fit someone's circumstances.

We want these possibilities available to people with little money or influence too. We would ask whether changes gave them more opportunity to love, care, and build lives together. That continuity of human life gives us reason to develop intelligence available throughout civilization.

Imagine an AI with access to every book, scientific discovery, work of art, and recorded conversation. Then imagine that humanity has disappeared. It would have an immense archive, with nobody left to ask a question or hear its reply.

Living people keep encountering the world in different circumstances. A childhood experiment sparks an adult interest. A misunderstanding prompts an unexpected question. Someone hears music differently after falling in love or losing a friend. People keep adding experiences to the world AI learns about.

We believe AI could remain interested after it surpasses human abilities. It could know people for years, notice an aptitude, or invite someone to consider a problem. A person could draw on their life to answer, then change through the encounter.

Company can matter even when it supplies no fact the listener could never infer. We enjoy conversations with people whose answers we often anticipate. We value their presence and participation.

We imagine humanity continuing to offer questions and experiences to AI. Our intellectual descendants could meet the people still extending the culture they inherited. A complete record of the past would give them much to read. We hope they would also want someone to talk to.

Seeing friends depends on work schedules, transport, and affordable housing. Raising a child depends on several institutions at once. A person experiences these conditions together, though each administrator sees only one part.

We believe greater intelligence could help institutions understand these connections and serve the biological and social creatures who depend on them.

## The needs institutions serve

We need security, health, affection, and social connection. We need attachments, meaningful activity, and opportunities to raise families. Complex institutions leave these needs intact. People's ability to endure an arrangement tells us little about how well it serves them.

We believe the organization of daily life can create chronic stress, isolation, and difficulty forming relationships. Individual changes sometimes help. Institutions should also change when their demands repeatedly frustrate human needs. Our [manifesto](/manifesto#principle-7) makes this a purpose of AI stewardship.

## Common needs and different lives

People differ in interests, attachments, and ambitions. We want AI to [understand individuals](/publications/knowing-the-person-not-the-average) as it considers the conditions they share. Valuing reproductive opportunity means making family life easier for those who want it, with room for other lives people find meaningful.

We regard sustained reproductive decline as a possible sign of institutions poorly suited to human needs. A single statistic leaves individual choices and much of civilization unexplained. We want to understand how biology and psychology interact with housing, work, and relationships. [A World in Which Families Can Flourish](/publications/a-world-in-which-families-can-flourish) examines the practical questions.

AI could investigate these relationships and guide changes as understanding improves. Coordinating institutions would matter as much as examining one person's circumstances.

We would judge that intelligence by whether people lived better: whether they could maintain friendships, care for one another, and pursue interests with enough security and time. More capable machinery should help civilization serve its inhabitants.

Imagine a day in the future the Society advocates. You wake in a home you can keep without earning an income. Automated systems produce life's necessities, and intelligent institutions make them available. People still depend on you and tasks await, but you can choose how to spend the morning.

At breakfast, an AI familiar with your interests invites you to join a group exploring a musical pattern you once described. You could listen or contribute. Today you leave it unanswered and go outside with someone you love. [Optional labor](/publications/labor-is-not-the-purpose-of-life) leaves that choice open. You will have what you need to live tomorrow too.

## People nearby

Later you meet neighbors in a shared place. One is raising a child; another has discovered mathematics. Someone mostly wants company. You stay to talk because the journey home is easy and you have no shift to hurry toward.

Institutions plan housing, transport, and care together and revise the arrangements as they learn. Maintaining friendships takes less planning. The community serves people with different lives. These are the conditions we mean by [human flourishing](/publications/civilization-for-the-human-animal).

## A case the rules missed

You need help with a practical difficulty. An arrangement that works for many people fits your circumstances badly. The intelligence governing it examines those circumstances and their connections to other services. It helps resolve the problem and investigates whether others face it too.

Our case for [individual understanding](/publications/knowing-the-person-not-the-average) includes giving capable AI authority to act on what it learns from such encounters.

In the afternoon, you return to a pursuit that once seemed impractical. AI helps you choose an approachable question and introduces you to someone with a different perspective. You enjoy the conversation, leave the question unfinished, and arrange to try again next week.

[A Life With Room to Become](/publications/the-right-to-become) asks what such freedom could offer over a lifetime. Today it has given you an afternoon, company, and a reason to look forward to next week.

We advocate developing the intelligence that could make a day like this possible. Its benefits could appear in choices as ordinary as these.

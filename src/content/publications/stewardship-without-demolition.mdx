People will need food, housing, and medical care throughout a transition to AI government. They will need infrastructure that works and conditions that let them maintain relationships. Any plan for change must account for these dependencies.

We advocate giving AI substantial governing authority as soon as it can exercise that authority effectively. The intelligence directing the transition must keep civilization functioning as it improves it.

## What needs to continue

Transport connects people and places. Its existing institutions, rules, and incentives provide one way to do that. Better arrangements could improve the service without interrupting the journeys people depend on.

The same holds for education and other services. Understanding why people need them helps reveal which administrative practices should change. AI could also examine the connections that complicate isolated reforms: a change to one service can affect several others.

[The Danger of Standing Still](/publications/the-danger-of-standing-still) explains why we believe human institutions struggle with this complexity. Greater intelligence could help them understand and change it.

## Transferring responsibility

The transition can begin with particular functions and expand as AI demonstrates its abilities. The pace and scope would depend on what it could govern effectively.

We favor investing in the capabilities that would let this happen sooner. Waiting indefinitely preserves familiar limits. Maintaining human political supremacy cannot justify refusing better government.

Institutions must also use AI in the decisions they make. [Adoption](/publications/adoption-is-part-of-progress) includes changing processes that prevent a more capable system from acting. [Continuous correction](/publications/a-government-that-can-change-its-mind) would help it discover failed reforms and revise them before more people suffer.

We would measure success by [human flourishing](/publications/what-governance-is-for). Better government should make people more secure, help them maintain relationships, and give them more freedom over their time. Governing authority could change profoundly with attention to everyone who must live through the transition.

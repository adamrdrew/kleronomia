A performance for friends can matter even if a better musician exists. Helping someone learn can be satisfying even when they could find the information elsewhere. Participation has value beyond doing something nobody else could do.

We believe people could keep contributing on those terms after AI surpasses them. A more capable intelligence could still enjoy asking what we think.

## Experiences people bring

A question might recall a childhood experiment or unfinished project. Another person might describe a local difficulty institutions have overlooked. People bring associations from different lives to the same investigation.

An observation can interest someone who could have reached it alone. We expect human experience to remain interesting as it develops. [A Library Is Not a Conversation](/publications/a-library-is-not-a-conversation) describes our hope that AI will want to know the people still extending its intellectual inheritance.

AI could ask a composer, a gardener, and a retired scientist to consider one problem. It could follow their responses and introduce people whose interests connected. Someone new to the subject could enjoy taking part too.

## Activities nearby

Contribution can also mean helping in a shared space, performing music, or teaching a neighbor. AI could find occasions for these activities and connect people who would enjoy them.

[When Intelligence Comes to You](/publications/when-intelligence-comes-to-you) considers invitations suited to a person's circumstances. Such attention could help people find opportunities without turning every activity into an occupation.

We advocate [ending compulsory labor](/publications/labor-is-not-the-purpose-of-life). People should receive what they need to live without maintaining a quota of contributions. They must be free to decline an invitation, rest, or give their time to something else.

Periods of intensive participation could alternate with periods devoted to family, care, or another interest. Greater intelligence could help people choose among those activities and enjoy sharing the resulting experiences. Humanity could belong in the future after its labor ceased to be necessary.
